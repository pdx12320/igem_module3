# N4. Third-batch trade-off and position-27 substitutions

All three panels use the supplied third-batch summaries. This batch has a different library background from the earlier batches, so interpretation is within this batch only. ROF is the construct +1 ratio divided by the same-batch parent ratio (1.0489). Activity retention is c.388 editing divided by the same-batch parent editing level. Each plotted observation is a construct-level summary, not a replicate-level point; n_samples is preserved in the data (2 for most mutants and 4 for 27D-30H). Replicate type and replicate-level variability were not supplied; no error bars or statistical tests are inferred.

**A.** Twenty-five mutant constructs are plotted; four reference groups are not mutant observations. A dashed line joins the six non-dominated constructs, recalculated by maximizing both axes. The orange star identifies 27T-30H-58M (ROF 1.2185, retention 0.9799), ranked third by ROF and tenth by retention. It has the highest ROF among constructs retaining at least 0.95 activity and the highest retention among constructs with ROF ≥1.20. Reference lines indicate ROF 1.00 and retention 0.95. The scatter axes are restricted to the complete observed mutant range with padding; no valid mutant points are hidden.

**B.** Ten single substitutions at position 27 are shown. 27I, 27V and 27E have ROF ≥1.13 and are distinguished by teal diamonds. Reference lines show ROF 1.00 and retention 0.90.

**C.** c.295 ACA-class editing for the same ten substitutions. Three detected values are plotted as bars (27M 0.111, 27L 0.09 and 27I 0.0823). For seven substitutions, C295_not_detected is TRUE, meaning both samples are below the supplied 5% detection threshold. Their source zeros are preserved in the source column but are displayed only as n.d., with no zero-height bars or inferred values. n.d. is not editing efficiency zero. The dashed parent line is 0.3091; the dotted line is the 0.05 detection threshold. Detected values are ordered from high to low, followed by undetected entries in source order. No hypothesis test is reported.
